How to play

    The goal

    Win everyone's chips. Make the best five-card hand from your two hole cards and the five community cards — or make everyone else fold.

    A hand of Hold'em

    • Blinds — the two players left of the dealer button post forced bets.
    • Pre-flop — everyone gets two private cards, then a betting round.
    • Flop, Turn, River — three, then one, then one community card, each followed by betting.
    • Showdown — remaining players reveal; the best hand wins the pot.

    Your options

    • Check — pass when there's no bet to you.
    • Call — match the current bet.
    • Bet / Raise — put in more; a raise must be at least the size of the previous bet or raise.
    • Fold — give up the hand.
    • All-in — bet everything. You can only win from each player what you put in; extra goes to side pots.

    Tips

    • Play fewer, stronger hands from early position; open up on the button.
    • Compare your odds of winning with the price of calling (the pot odds).
    • When your stack drops below ~12 big blinds, going all-in or folding is often best.
